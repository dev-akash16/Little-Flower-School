Early Childhood

Pre-Primary Wing (Playgroup, Nursery, LKG, UKG)

At Little Flower School, we believe that children learn best when learning feels like joyful play. Our early childhood classrooms are designed with bright colors, sensory play items, storytelling corners, and clay modeling activities.

  • Phonics & Pre-Reading: Letter sound association, rhymes, and conversational English.
  • Sensory & Motor Skills: Clay modeling, colouring, paper folding, and basic physical coordination.
  • Numeracy Foundations: Pattern recognition, counting beads, sorting, and shape identification.
Enroll Your Little One
Classes I to V

Primary School Wing

The primary years bridge basic skills into conceptual understanding. Children delve deeper into communicative English, Hindi, Mathematics, Environmental Studies, and Moral Science, supplemented by regular creative projects.

  • Environmental Stewardship: Hands-on plantation drives, "Save Water" poster competitions.
  • Language & Public Speaking: Recitation, reading hour, morning assembly speeches, and drama.
  • Experiential Mathematics: Conceptual arithmetic, geometry puzzles, and mental math practice.
Primary Admission Info
Classes VI to VIII

Middle School Wing

Middle schoolers transition into independent, critical learners. We foster scientific curiosity through experiments, digital skills with foundational computer education, and character through peer leadership.

  • Subject Specialization: Physics, Chemistry, Biology, History, Civics, Geography.
  • Digital Literacy: Computer skills, logical problem solving, and modern information access.
  • Leadership & Sports: House competitions, student council participation, track and field events.
