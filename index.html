About Our Institution

Fostering Knowledge, Character & Lifelong Curiosity

"Learning Today for Leading Tomorrow" — Building strong foundations for every child.

Located at Kutubpur Kothi in Hajipur, Little Flower School is dedicated to delivering quality English medium education that nurtures the intellect, spirit, and creative potential of each learner. Our school provides a secure, caring, and disciplined campus where values meet modern pedagogy.

Infrastructure & Amenities

Designed for Safe, Joyful & Holistic Learning

From spacious classrooms to safe bus transport, we ensure every child enjoys comfortable, secure schooling.

Spacious Classrooms

Naturally lit, airy, and equipped with ergonomic seating to ensure comfortable learning throughout the school day.

Safe Bus Transportation

Reliable and monitored school bus fleet catering to Kutubpur Kothi, Hajipur town, and neighboring residential areas.
