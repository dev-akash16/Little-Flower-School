Our Foundation & Mission

"Learning Today for Leading Tomorrow"

Little Flower School was founded with the conviction that true education cultivates both intellectual capability and moral integrity. Situated at Kutubpur Kothi in Hajipur, our campus is a sanctuary of disciplined learning and character development.

Our Vision & Educational Philosophy

At Little Flower School, we envision empowering every young child into an enlightened, responsible, and compassionate citizen. We focus on conceptual clarity rather than rote memorization, encouraging students to ask questions, experiment, and express themselves freely.

We believe that early school years form the bedrock of human character. With attentive teachers and an engaging environment, we inspire our learners to strive for excellence in academics, co-curricular arts, sports, and community service.

Our Guiding Pillars

01

Discipline & Integrity

Instilling self-discipline, ethical values, punctuality, and mutual respect in daily conduct.

02

Academic Rigour

Robust curriculum fostering mathematical reasoning, English proficiency, and scientific mindset.

03

Eco-Consciousness

Empowering children to care for the environment through tree planting and Swachhata drives.

04

Holistic Growth

Nurturing public speaking, arts, creative hobbies, physical fitness, and teamwork.

Faculty Excellence

Empowered & Passionate Educators

Our teachers undergo periodic professional development programs, pedagogical workshops, and student-centered training to stay abreast of the best teaching methodologies.

With small classroom batches and an optimal student-to-teacher ratio (15:1), every child receives individual mentorship, constructive encouragement, and emotional care.
