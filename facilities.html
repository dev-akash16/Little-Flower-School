Academic Tech

💻 Smart Digital Classrooms

Classrooms are equipped with interactive audiovisual smart displays, digital projectors, and multimedia educational software. Concepts in science, mathematics, and geography are brought to life through 2D/3D visualizations and engaging animations.

Interactive digital syllabus modules for all grades
Ergonomic benches, child-safe furniture & ample natural ventilation
Optimal teacher-to-student ratio for focused personal attention
Hands-On STEM

🔬 Science & Discovery Laboratory

Experiential learning is the cornerstone of scientific mastery. Our laboratory is furnished with practical demonstration kits, optical microscopes, specimen slides, glassware, and physics measurement instruments strictly supervised by experienced science faculty.

Physics, Chemistry & Biology apparatus adhering to safety codes
Guided weekly practical sessions for Middle Wing students
Annual Science Exhibition and innovation model making
Digital Skills

🖥️ Computer & IT Education Lab

Preparing children for the digital era from early primary school. The air-cooled computer lab features modern desktop systems with high-speed internet, secure firewalls, touch typing software, and foundational coding (Scratch, Python logic, and office productivity tools).

1:1 computer workstation availability during lab periods
Child-safe internet filtering and supervised browsing
Cyber safety awareness and digital citizenship workshops
Resource Center

📚 Library & Knowledge Resource Center

A serene haven cultivating the love of reading. Our library houses over 2,500+ curated volumes ranging from classic literature, moral storybooks (Panchatantra, Jataka Tales), children's encyclopedias, to competitive exam foundation series and periodicals.

Dedicated weekly library reading hour for every class
Bilingual collections in both Hindi and English
Book borrowing facility to encourage home reading habits
Athletics & Fitness

⚽ Sports Ground & Play Arena

Physical education is vital for mental vigor and sportsmanship. Our campus grounds support outdoor sports including badminton, cricket practice nets, kho-kho, football, volleyball, yoga, and gymnastics, alongside dedicated play equipment for kindergarten.

Dedicated Physical Education (PT) instructors and coaches
Inter-house athletics tournaments and Annual Sports Day
Child-friendly rubberized swings and slides for junior kids
Safe Transit

🚌 Safe & Reliable School Transport

Hassle-free daily commute for students across Hajipur and surrounding localities. Our fleet of verified school vans is driven by background-checked drivers, accompanied by responsible attendants, and adheres to all school vehicle safety norms.

Covers Kutubpur, Ramashish Chowk, Anjanpir, Konhara Ghat & nearby routes
Dedicated female helper attendants for junior students
First-aid kit and emergency protocols inside every vehicle
Health & Hygiene

💧 Pure RO Water & Sanitized Campus

Child health and sanitation are our topmost daily priority. The campus is equipped with multi-stage Reverse Osmosis (RO) drinking water coolers, regular TDS testing, and clean, gender-segregated sanitization blocks maintained on an hourly cleaning schedule.

Multi-stage industrial RO filtration with regular water lab testing
Separate, hygienic washroom complexes for boys and girls
Daily sanitization of high-contact banisters, desks and doors
Safety & Vigilance

🛡️ 24x7 CCTV Surveillance & Gated Security

Parents enjoy complete peace of mind when their children are on campus. The entire school boundary, corridors, stairwells, gates, and common gathering areas are under round-the-clock high-definition CCTV monitoring with dedicated security guards.

HD CCTV cameras covering corridors, gates & playground
Strict visitor verification and gate-pass logging system
Authorized guardian pickup identification protocols
Creative Arts

🎭 Arts, Music & Cultural Activity Center

Nurturing imagination beyond textbooks. Our cultural hall provides a vibrant stage for public speaking, speech competitions, singing, keyboard, tabla, theater dramatics, classical dance forms, painting, and fancy dress celebrations.

Acoustic multi-purpose hall with sound system and stage
Guidance in vocal music, instruments, and classical dance
Regular celebrations for Independence Day, Republic Day & Annual Day
