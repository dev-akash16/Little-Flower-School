Give Your Child the Best Educational Start

Little Flower School welcomes admissions from Nursery through Grade VIII. We maintain limited class sizes to ensure personalized care, active mentor interaction, and a loving educational atmosphere.

Simple 4-Step Process:

1
Obtain & Fill Enquiry Form:

Fill out the online enquiry form or collect the prospectus from our Kutubpur Kothi office.

2
Parent & Child Interaction:

An informal, welcoming meeting to get acquainted with the parents and understand the child's strengths.

3
Document Submission:

Submit required documentation (birth certificate, photos, previous report cards).

4
Seat Confirmation:

Receive enrollment confirmation, uniform specifications, and academic calendar.

Documents Checklist for Admission

1. Birth Certificate

Self-attested photocopy of Birth Certificate issued by Municipal Corporation / competent authority.

2. Passport Photos

4 recent color passport-sized photographs of the student and 2 of each parent/guardian.

3. Transfer Certificate (TC)

Original Transfer Certificate and Report Card from the previous recognized school (Class 2 onwards).

4. Aadhar / ID Proof

Photocopy of student's Aadhar card (if available) and residential address proof of parents.
